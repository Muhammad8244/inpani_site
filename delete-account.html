Delete your account

Applies to the Inpani (customer) and Inpani Driver apps

You can ask us to delete your Inpani account and the personal data held with it at any time. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need to be signed in to make the request.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address or phone number on the account, so we can tell it is really you:

Email support@inpani.pk

Tell us:

  1. Which app — Inpani or Inpani Driver
  2. The phone number or Google account you sign in with
  3. That you want the account deleted

You can also call or message +92 331 5242754.

How long it takes. We confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming. If we cannot verify that the request came from the account holder, we will tell you rather than delete someone else's data.

What we keep, and why

A completed delivery involves two people. When one of them deletes their account, we detach their name, phone number and address from the order but keep the record of the delivery itself — the tanker size, the price agreed, the date and the rating given.

We do this because the other party is entitled to their own history: a driver's earnings record and reputation must not disappear because a customer closed their account, and a customer's record of what they paid must survive a driver leaving. These retained records no longer identify you.

We keep them for up to 3 years for tax and dispute purposes, after which they are removed entirely.

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling Inpani removes it from your phone but does not delete your account or the data on our servers. Use the email above if you want the account itself removed.

Just want your data, not deletion?

Email the same address and ask for a copy of what we hold about you, and we will send it. Full detail on what the apps collect is in our Privacy Policy.
